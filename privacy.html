Fitnest Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Fitnest Coach is a fitness coaching application that allows personal trainers and their clients to manage workout plans, nutrition plans, progress, check-ins, photos, messages, and related fitness information.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as name and email address.
  • Profile information.
  • Workout and training information.
  • Nutrition plans and meal records.
  • Body measurements and progress information.
  • Daily and weekly check-in information.
  • Photos voluntarily uploaded by users.
  • Messages exchanged between trainers and clients.
  • Technical information necessary to operate the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the Fitnest Coach service.
  • Allow trainers to manage their clients.
  • Allow clients to follow training and nutrition plans.
  • Track fitness progress and check-ins.
  • Enable communication between trainers and clients.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the application.
  • Improve the application and user experience.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary for operating the application, such as cloud hosting and email delivery providers.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information stored and transmitted through Fitnest Coach.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

User Choices and Data Deletion

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information by contacting us at the email address below.

Uploaded fitness information, check-ins, photos, and other account data may also be deleted as part of an account deletion request.

Children's Privacy

Fitnest Coach is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law without appropriate parental or guardian consent.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
